Production quality & evaluation

Test software and AI systems against agreed requirements, and prepare them for release and daily operation.

A prototype or existing system needs clearer tests, release criteria, or operational ownership.

Describe what needs to be release-ready ↗

Who this fits

Teams assessing a prototype, planned release or existing system against requirements for real use.

What the work can cover

Identify risks and test against intended use; define review/release criteria and responsibilities; prepare monitoring and incident-response plans appropriate to the agreed scope.

Typical deliverables

A menu to agree in scope, rather than a fixed package.

  • A risk assessment tied to the system and its intended use
  • Tests for functionality, reliability, access controls, and relevant AI failure cases
  • Review and release checklists with named decision owners
  • A monitoring and incident-response plan with escalation steps

How an engagement can work

  1. Agree requirements and risks
  2. inspect existing evidence
  3. run scoped tests/evaluations
  4. report findings and evidence gaps
  5. prepare a release/operation decision with owners

What we need to understand

  • The system design, intended users, and planned uses
  • Existing policies and relevant requirements from your advisers
  • Known failure cases and the people responsible for review

Start with a description. Agree data handling and access before sharing confidential materials.

Expected outcome and evidence

An evidence-based decision, including reasons to hold a release. Evaluate coverage of agreed risks, reproducible findings and clarity of ownership; passing checks establishes only what was tested.

Choose measures and acceptance criteria together before building. These are aims and evaluation questions, not guaranteed results.

Scope and constraints

Assessment does not certify legal compliance, eliminate all failures or guarantee safe operation. A monitoring plan does not mean Kheya operates monitoring or provides an incident-response service.

Deliverables, access, responsibilities, schedule, pricing, IP terms, and any further support belong in the agreed scope.

Common questions

Must we have an AI system?

This applies to software as well as AI.

Can evaluation find that we should not release?

Yes; unresolved failures and missing evidence belong in the decision.

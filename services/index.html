How an engagement can unfold

A clear decision at every stage

This is an example, not a fixed package. Advisory may stop at a recommendation. Building, deployment and handover apply only when included in the agreed scope.

  1. 01

    Direction

    Understand your business goals, the people doing the work, and where software can help.

    Possible output, when in scope
    Opportunity map and risk review
    • Workflow review
    • Use-case shortlist
    • Success criteria
  2. 02

    Decision

    Compare approaches, tools, and costs. Decide what to build, buy, or leave alone.

    Possible output, when in scope
    Technical blueprint and cost model
    • Architecture options
    • Build-or-buy decision
    • Delivery scope
  3. 03

    Development

    Build the software, connect the right systems, and test against agreed success criteria.

    Possible output, when in scope
    Tested workflows and production code
    • Working integrations
    • Evaluation suite
    • Technical documentation
  4. 04

    Deployment

    Prepare the system and its owners for daily use, with monitoring and a clear way to handle problems.

    Possible output, when in scope
    Monitoring plan and operating guide
    • Access controls
    • Monitoring checks
    • Team handover

Before the work begins

A shared definition of done

Agree the exact scope, responsibilities and acceptance criteria before work begins.

  • The product or workflow, users, and intended outcome
  • Approved data access, integrations, and human review
  • How the result will be tested and accepted
  • Documentation, operating ownership and any separately agreed support

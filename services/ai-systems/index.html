Who this fits

Teams evaluating language, search, document-processing, assistant or decision-support tasks.

What the work can cover

Assess the use case and approved data; design and prototype a scoped system; define evaluation, access, update handling and human review. Source references apply where the use case requires them.

Typical deliverables

A menu to agree in scope, rather than a fixed package.

  • A use-case assessment, data review, and design for the agreed AI system
  • A scoped AI prototype, with source references where the use case requires them
  • Data access controls, update handling, and human review steps
  • An evaluation set for output quality, failure cases, and unsupported claims

How an engagement can work

  1. Define the task and baseline
  2. inspect data suitability
  3. prototype
  4. evaluate representative and difficult cases
  5. document limits and decide the next step

What we need to understand

  • Representative data or documents approved for the project
  • Examples of the task and what a useful result looks like
  • Data owners, access rules, and review requirements

Start with a description. Agree data handling and access before sharing confidential materials.

Expected outcome and evidence

Evidence of whether AI helps this task. Evaluate relevant accuracy, source support, failure handling and reviewer effort; retain unsupported or unsuccessful results in the assessment.

Choose measures and acceptance criteria together before building. These are aims and evaluation questions, not guaranteed results.

Scope and constraints

A prototype is not a release-ready system. AI outputs can be wrong; review requirements depend on intended use. No model, accuracy rate or autonomous decision authority is promised here.

Deliverables, access, responsibilities, schedule, pricing, IP terms, and any further support belong in the agreed scope.

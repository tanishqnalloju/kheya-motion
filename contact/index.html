A few practical questions

Do we need a complete project brief before talking?

No. Start with a business problem, a product idea or a system that needs to improve. The first job is to understand the work and decide what approach makes sense, including whether AI can help.

Can we start with a small project?

Yes. A defined feature, workflow or system upgrade makes it easier to agree on scope, test the result and decide what comes next. Advisory or a prototype can clarify the direction before a larger build.

How are cost and timing decided?

They depend on the scope, data, integrations and review requirements. Agree the deliverables, assumptions, responsibilities and acceptance criteria before committing to a build.

Agree the boundaries before work begins

Data access and handling need agreement with the relevant owners. Delivery arrangements, responsibilities, commercial terms and any support are scoped for the engagement.
